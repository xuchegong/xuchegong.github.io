About News Research Teaching CV

I study how farmers make risk management decisions, with a focus on crop insurance.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Agricultural Economics at Mississippi State University. I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural Economics from Iowa State University in 2024.

My work combines microeconomic theory, behavioral economics, and farm-level survey and administrative data to understand why farmers choose the coverage they do, and what those choices mean for farm income, program design, and the environment.

  • Behavioral economics of risk management
  • Crop insurance design and policy
  • Farmer decision-making under risk
  • Agricultural and environmental policy
