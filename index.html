I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Agricultural Economics at Mississippi State University. I received my Ph.D. in Agricultural Economics from Iowa State University in 2024.

My research studies how farmers make risk management decisions, with a focus on crop insurance. I combine microeconomic theory, behavioral economics, and farm-level survey and administrative data to understand why farmers choose the coverage they do, and what those choices mean for farm income, program design, and the environment.

Research interests: behavioral economics of agricultural risk management, crop insurance design and policy, farmer decision-making under risk, and agricultural production and environmental policy.
