Break-Even Reference Dependence in Crop Insurance Decisions: Theory and Evidence
with Hongli Feng, David A. Hennessy, Jennifer Ifft, Robert Shupp, and Michel Regenwetter
Conditionally Accepted, American Journal of Agricultural Economics

Summary

Farmers who treat breaking even as a reference point should cluster at the coverage level that secures ex ante break-even at the lowest premium cost. The paper develops this prediction and examines it using a survey of Iowa and Kansas corn growers.

Presentations: SCC-76 Annual Meeting (2025); University of Delaware, Department of Applied Economics and Statistics (2024); Mississippi State University, Department of Agricultural Economics (2024); Iowa State University, Department of Economics (2024)

Reframing Underinsurance: Contract Displays and Coverage Choices in the U.S. Federal Crop Insurance Program
with Hongli Feng, David A. Hennessy, Jennifer Ifft, and Robert Shupp

Summary

A randomized information experiment with Iowa and Kansas corn growers shows that adding outcome-distribution and indemnity figures to the standard feature-based enrollment table raises coverage demand among underinsured farmers.

Presentations: Center for Behavioral & Experimental Agri-Environmental Research (2025); SCC-76 Annual Meeting (2025); AAEA Annual Meeting (2022, poster)
